# Editing a wish is unrestricted

An owner may change any field of any wish at any time, including one people have already paid towards — the same stance we take on deleting (a profile is the owner's to curate), and nothing about a contribution is recomputed from a wish's fields, so an edit cannot move money.

## Considered Options

Freezing the price once the first paid contribution arrives, flooring it at what has already been received, and freezing a funded wish entirely. All three buy a contributor a guarantee we do not otherwise offer them — there is no record of what a wish looked like when they gave — and all three cost a second card and dialog state, on exactly the wishes people care most about. A typo in a funded wish would become unfixable except by deleting it.

## Consequences

A wish's price is a target, not a commitment: an owner may retitle «پلی‌استیشن ۵» to something else after money has landed, and a contributor has no way to see that it happened. We accept that in exchange for one rule with no states in it. If this is ever revisited, the thing to add first is history — a contributor can only be protected by a record of what they gave towards, not by locking fields.
