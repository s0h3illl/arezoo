# Dashboard

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

`.scratch/dashboard/spec.md`, ready-for-agent, in the shape of `.scratch/wish-detail/spec.md` — Problem Statement, Solution, User Stories, Implementation Decisions, Testing Decisions, Out of Scope — with numbered issues beside it under `issues/`. The signed-in user's own section of the app: three pages at `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/messages` and `/dashboard/withdrawals`, plus the header account menu that reaches them. Every conflict between the design and the domain resolved on paper; build tickets are added once the spec exists.

## Notes

**Domain:** Arezoo — a wishlist where people publish wishes and others help pay for them. `CONTEXT.md` holds the language; `docs/adr/` holds the decisions. ADR-0003 (history outlives the wish), ADR-0005 (balance is derived, never stored), ADR-0006 (earned money is held three days) and ADR-0010 (Visibility hides identity, never amount) all bear on this section.

**The word «Profile» is spent.** `CONTEXT.md` gives it to the *public* page at `/u/{username}`. This section is the **Dashboard** — private, at `/dashboard`, behind `auth` + `verified`. `admin.dashboard` keeps its name; the two are separated by namespace, exactly as `App\Http\Controllers\Admin\UserController` is separated from a root one. Nothing here is ever called a profile.

**Three other efforts get amended by this one, and so is the domain language.** None of it is optional and none of it should be made silently:

- `.scratch/balance-and-withdrawals/spec.md` — stories 8–10 promise an itemised statement of the contributions behind the balance. This map removes it. That spec keeps everything else: the available/held derivation, the fee, the hold period, the one-open-request rule, and the whole admin queue. **This map does not re-open any of it.**
- `.scratch/auth-pages/` — **one amendment.** `auth/VerifyEmail.vue` grows the email-change form, so a registration typo stops being unrecoverable. `auth/Login.vue` and `auth/ForgotPassword.vue` are untouched, and all three keep their green `status` panels — see the amendment on [What the shell does with a server `status`](issues/10-what-the-shell-does-with-a-server-status.md).
- `.scratch/contributions/` — the contribute form gains `max:512` on `message`, validated before the gateway redirect, and should tell the contributor that the owner reads their message whatever its visibility. This map records both as constraints; it builds neither.
- `CONTEXT.md:38` and a new **ADR** — [What the inbox shows](issues/04-what-the-inbox-shows.md) reversed the rule that a message is narrower than identity. The line is rewritten and the reversal recorded as a decision, alongside ADR-0010.

**Skills to consult:** `/grilling` and `/domain-modeling` for the decision tickets; `/prototype` for the menu ticket; `/research` for the research ticket. Laravel Boost's `search-docs` before any framework-shaped decision, and `laravel-best-practices`, `inertia-vue-development`, `fortify-development` and `pest-testing` where they apply.

**Standing preferences for this effort:**

- Plan, don't do. No route, controller, migration or component lands from this map — the spec is the deliverable.
- Prefer what the app already does. `PUT /user/profile-information` and `PUT /user/password` already exist with custom Fortify actions; `reka-ui` is already a dependency; `Inertia::scroll()` + `<InfiniteScroll>` is the established list pattern; `UsernameValidationRules` is already extracted for reuse. Reach for a new mechanism only when a ticket records why the existing one fails.
- Masking is a server concern. A message the reader may not see must not reach the browser at all, the standard `wish-detail` set for contributor rows.
- The environment's limits are load-bearing, not incidental. There is no `gd` extension, and the Pest browser cannot POST multipart. Any decision about avatars has to survive both.

## Decisions so far

- [The destination and the shape of the dashboard](issues/00-charting.md) — three pages at `/dashboard` (account info), `/dashboard/messages` (contribution messages) and `/dashboard/withdrawals`, ~~all behind `auth` + `verified`~~ — **superseded by [How the profile form is tested](issues/09-how-the-profile-form-is-tested.md)**: `/dashboard` is the exception, on `auth` only, so an unverified user can reach the account screen and correct a mistyped address; the other two keep `auth` + `verified`; no `DashboardLayout` — they are ordinary `AppLayout` pages, and the only chrome change is a `reka-ui` dropdown under the header's user icon, which also gives the app its first sign-out. The account screen edits name, username, email, avatar, bio and password. Email is edited on the same form as everything else, through stock Fortify. The inbox carries only `paid` contributions that have a message ~~and whose visibility is `public` or `owner`~~ — **the visibility half is superseded by [What the inbox shows](issues/04-what-the-inbox-shows.md)**, which admits `hidden` rows masked as «ناشناس». `users.messages_seen_at` drives an unread badge. No password confirmation anywhere — a deliberate, recorded risk.

- [How an avatar is carried](issues/01-how-an-avatar-is-carried.md) — the browser downscales before posting: a canvas centre-crops to 512×512 WebP, which needs no `gd`, strips the EXIF GPS that would otherwise be served from a public profile, and keeps a contributor list from shipping megabytes for forty-pixel circles. The server validates bytes only (`image`, `max:512`) — no `dimensions` rule, since nothing server-side ever decodes the image. The column holds a path under `avatars/`; a new `User::avatarUrl` accessor converts it once, so `UserResource`, `ContributionResource` and every frontend type keep the shape they already have. Avatar and bio ride with name on `PUT /user/profile-information` (spoofed `POST`, `forceFormData`, a `remove_avatar` boolean), and `WishController::thumbnailChange()`'s store/replace/delete shape transfers whole. **Known gap:** the canvas downscale has no automated test — the Pest browser cannot POST multipart.

- [How an email change is carried](issues/02-how-an-email-change-is-carried.md) — **superseded and rewritten mid-effort.** Stock Fortify carries it: `UpdateUserProfileInformation` keeps `email` and both its branches, writing the new address immediately, nulling `email_verified_at` and mailing the standard verification notification. No `EmailChangeController`, no signed URL, no `sha1` replay fingerprint, no new notification — the earlier design bought one recoverable failure for a controller, two routes and a mail. The recovery path holds because `user-profile-information.update` runs on `['web', 'auth']`, not `verified`, so `auth/VerifyEmail.vue` can carry the same form — and **[How the profile form is tested](issues/09-how-the-profile-form-is-tested.md) widened it**: `/dashboard` itself drops `verified`, so the account screen is the primary door and the verify notice's form is a kept convenience, not the only way in. **Two accepted risks:** an unproven address is written, so a typo costs the verified status until corrected from the verify notice; and the old address is never notified, so with no password confirmation a hijacked session moves it in silence.

- [What changing a username costs](issues/03-what-changing-a-username-costs.md) — **one form**: name, username, email, avatar and bio all post to `PUT /user/profile-information`, password keeps `PUT /user/password`. Nothing is shown on submit — no dialog, no confirmation, and no warning copy anywhere, so Arezoo does not tell a user that renaming breaks the profile links they have shared (wish links are id-based and survive). The freed username is takeable immediately: `CONTEXT.md:29` stands, no hold, no undo, and no rate limit, since a name only frees when its holder renames. The real find: `validateUsernameIsFree()` counts the user's own row, so a no-op save fails today — `usernameRules()` gains an optional user to ignore, `CreateNewUser` unchanged. `RESERVED_USERNAMES` already exists and gains `support`, `help`, `arezoo`.

- [What the inbox shows, and how much of it loads](issues/04-what-the-inbox-shows.md) — **charting's visibility filter is reversed**: the inbox carries every `paid` contribution with a message at **all three** visibilities, `hidden` included, masked as «ناشناس» exactly as a contributor row is. `CONTEXT.md:38` is therefore wrong and must be rewritten, and the reversal needs its own ADR beside ADR-0010 — a message is free text, so masking the name does not mask the body, and `hidden` now means "my name is not printed", not "the owner learns nothing". Ordered `settled_at` desc (not `created_at` — it is the column the row displays), `id` desc; `Inertia::scroll()` at **15** per page, sized for a message list rather than the card grid's 7. A row carries the message in full (no clamp), name and avatar per `ContributorRow.vue` with no profile link and no contributor `id`, the wish title as plain text that **never** links — so a trashed wish needs no marker — the amount (ADR-0010) and a relative `settled_at`. A new **`MessageResource`**; `ContributionResource` is untouched, keeping `wish-detail`'s "no message key anywhere" structurally true. One empty state covering both cases. «کاربر حذف‌شده» settled and blocked on user soft-deletion. `/wishes/{wish}` unchanged. **Constraint recorded for the contribute effort:** `message` is `max:512`, validated before the gateway redirect.

- [The account menu in the header](issues/06-the-account-menu-in-the-header.md) — **variant B, the identity card**: a `reka-ui` `DropdownMenu` whose trigger is the user's 38px round avatar (initial-letter circle as fallback), opening on a name / `@username` block, then صفحه من / اطلاعات من / پیام‌ها / برداشت‌ها, a separator, and خروج از حساب. Sign-out is **`<Link :href="logout()">`** — Wayfinder's `logout()` already carries `method: 'post'`, and an Inertia `<Form>` inside the menu **does not work**: the item's select unmounts the portal before it submits. An unverified user sees an amber «ایمیلت هنوز تأیید نشده» strip and ~~the three `/dashboard/*` rows **disabled**~~ — **amended by [How the profile form is tested](issues/09-how-the-profile-form-is-tested.md)**: اطلاعات من is **enabled for everyone**, since `/dashboard` no longer requires verification; پیام‌ها and برداشت‌ها stay disabled — this closes the map's fog item on the subject. The badge is a **dot** on the avatar and a **count** on the پیام‌ها row. `AppLayout` gains one **`<ConfigProvider dir="rtl">`**: `reka-ui` reads direction from the provider, not the DOM `dir`, so `align="end"` flips without it. **No burger** — the dropdown is already the mobile answer, and `AdminHeader`'s burger exists only because it renders six items inline. `auth/VerifyEmail.vue` keeps its own خروج link. Prototype on branch `prototype/dashboard-account-menu`.

- [How the profile form is tested](issues/09-how-the-profile-form-is-tested.md) — **no test re-asserts Fortify**: the write, the nulled `email_verified_at`, the verification mail and the `name`/`email` validation are vendor behaviour, so `Notification::fake()` appears nowhere and the email branch gets no feature test. Two seams: the app-added rules at the HTTP boundary as `RegisterTest.php` already does, the browser reserved for real interactions. **Six feature tests** — five update-only username cases (the no-op save, a case-only self-rename, a rename onto a held name, a rename onto a reserved name, a freed name immediately taken) plus one asserting an *unverified* user can PUT the endpoint, the middleware fact the recovery path rests on; shape, length, charset and Persian copy stay pinned once at register. The ticket's "one entry point test or two" **dissolves at the HTTP seam** — one route, byte-identical requests. **No unit test on `usernameRules()`**: both call shapes already run through a real endpoint, and asserting a returned rule array is asserting implementation. **Five browser tests** — the account screen saving name/username/bio, the email change reaching the row, the verify-notice form proving the *second* form is wired, a validation error actually rendering, and a `/dashboard` smoke test. **Not tested, deliberately:** the avatar in either seam (the known gap from ticket 01) and the password form. Nothing tests this endpoint today — `grep` finds no file — so no existing test is kept or deleted. **This ticket amended the map's route policy — see below.**

- [What the shell does with a server `status`](issues/10-what-the-shell-does-with-a-server-status.md) — **decided, then overruled; the standing answer is the amendment.** The decision centralised success messages in `AppLayout` on Inertia v3 flash data, deleted the shared `status` prop, rebound five Fortify responses into a new `lang/fa/fortify.php`, and moved two auth pages off their green panels behind Layout Props. **The user reversed it, and set the rule instead: wherever Fortify forces a `status`, use `status` and change nothing; where the endpoint is our own code, raise a toast.** So `share()` is unedited, no response is rebound, and the three auth pages keep their panels and their sentinel. Checked against `route:list`, **every save this map adds is a Fortify save** — name, username, email, bio and the avatar all post to `PUT /user/profile-information`, the password form to `PUT /user/password`, the inbox is read-only — so **the dashboard raises no toast at all**, and the toast half of the rule governs our own controllers, today only `WishController@store`. The account screen shows `status` and **maps `profile-information-updated` and `password-updated` to Persian in the Vue file**, as `auth/VerifyEmail.vue:34` already does with its own sentinel; the raw constants are English. **Two costs accepted, not solved:** a shared prop is restored by a Back navigation, and a repeat save re-flashes an identical string — today's auth-page behaviour, now the dashboard's. **[The shell says «saved»](issues/11-the-shell-says-saved.md) is closed as won't-do**, and [How the profile form is tested](issues/09-how-the-profile-form-is-tested.md) reverts to one browser assertion.

## Out of scope

- **The balance arithmetic and everything under it.** Available/held derivation, the fee, the hold period, the one-open-request rule, the concurrency lock, and the entire admin withdrawals queue belong to `.scratch/balance-and-withdrawals/spec.md`. This map decides only what the owner-facing page shows and where the bank-details form sits.
- **The itemised balance statement.** Ruled out at charting: the owner sees the figures, the request form and the request history, not the contributions behind them. Removes stories 8–10 from the balance spec.
- **A dashboard overview screen.** `/dashboard` is the account-info screen, not a landing page of summary cards. Considered and cut at charting as a fourth surface nobody asked for.
- **Managing wishes from the dashboard.** Adding, editing and deleting a wish already happen on the public profile at `/u/{username}`; the account menu links there («صفحه من») and changes nothing about it.
- **Replying to a message.** The inbox is read-only. There is no messaging between users anywhere in the app and this section does not start one.
- **Password confirmation on sensitive actions.** Decided against at charting, to keep this effort free of a dependency on the unbuilt `.scratch/auth-pages/issues/14-confirm-password-page.md`. The accepted consequence is that an open session can change the Sheba and then withdraw. Recorded as a risk in the spec, not as a gap to close later.
- **Enforcing the verified-email policy on `wishes.store`.** `.scratch/auth-pages/spec.md` states that publishing a Wish requires verification; the route carries only `auth` today. A real gap, and that effort's to close.
- **Email notifications of any kind.** No mail when a message arrives, when a withdrawal is paid, or when a withdrawal is rejected. The screens are the only notification, as the balance spec already ruled.
